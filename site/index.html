Workout+

The Karoo 3 workout layout, on your Karoo 2.

A free extension that takes over the Karoo 2's workout page while a workout runs: the target bar, a big interval countdown with the interval graph, your CORE heat data and the fields you choose. Your other ride pages stay as they are.

Reading the target

The same colors as the Karoo 3. The box slides along the bar: the middle third is your target range. Single-value targets get the Karoo's own band, ±5 % for power and ±7.5 % for heart rate.

Set it up

You need a computer with adb and USB debugging turned on on the Karoo (Hammerhead's guide).

  1. Download the APK

    Get karoo-workout-plus.apk from the latest release.

  2. Install it and let it draw over the ride app

    adb install -r karoo-workout-plus.apk
adb shell appops set com.angkyria.karooworkout SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow

    Or open Workout+ on the Karoo and tap Grant.

  3. Ride a workout

    Start a ride with a workout loaded. Workout+ covers the workout page by itself. To try it at your desk first, turn on demo mode in its settings.

Good to know

Your buttons stay yours

Workout+ never takes the hardware buttons. Page changes, lap and back work as always, and a swipe changes the page underneath.

Choose your fields

Four slots: power, heart rate, cadence, lap and time data, core temperature and more. Set any of them to None to give the rest more room.

Power from your sensor

Your power, heart rate or cadence come from the Karoo's workout data, or straight from the sensor when the workout data has none.

CORE heat

With a CORE sensor, core temperature, heat strain and skin temperature show in the heat-zone colors. The heat strain comes from the CORE Heat extension.

Pause and skip stay on the Karoo

The extension API can't pause or skip a workout, so those stay on the Karoo's own controls. Workout+ shows the result, and PAUSED while the ride is paused.

Intervals as you ride them

The Karoo shares the running interval only, so the graph fills in as you ride. What's still to come is hatched.
